Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Does DineFlow support both AC and Non-AC GST tax slabs?

Yes! DineFlow includes pre-configured Indian GST tax engines that automatically apply 5% GST (2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST) for restaurant food service, 18% for alcoholic beverages/outdoor catering, and supports exemption codes for raw grocery items.

Can we use our existing ESC/POS thermal receipt printers?

Absolutely. DineFlow supports all standard 80mm and 58mm thermal printers (USB, Ethernet, Bluetooth, and Wi-Fi) from Epson, TVS-E, NGX, Everycom, and Star Micronics with customized Indian KOT & Tax Invoice formatting.

How does the raw material inventory depletion work?

Whenever a dish is ordered (e.g., Chicken Biryani), DineFlow's recipe mapping automatically depletes the exact raw ingredients (e.g., 250g Chicken, 180g Basmati Rice, 30g Ghee, Spices) from that branch's inventory in real time, alerting the manager before stock runs out.

Can I switch between roles seamlessly in the ERP?

Yes! The top navigation bar and sidebar in DineFlow contain an instant Role Switcher dropdown and user profile controls allowing authorized staff to navigate between all 10 role dashboards with one click.
